        Before you start

        LeadQualify analyzes the LinkedIn or Sales Navigator page you are currently viewing and sends that content to your configured AI provider (Groq) to score it against your ICP. It never browses, connects, or messages on your behalf, and only runs on LinkedIn pages you open.

        • Automating or scraping LinkedIn may violate its User Agreement — you are responsible for how you use this tool.
        • Scraped page data is sent to Groq for scoring; scores are cached locally in your browser.
        • Use it for prospect research you're permitted to perform.
